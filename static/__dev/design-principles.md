Distinctive thread: Organic Night Market — a warm, dark charcoal canvas illuminated by fresh mint accents and vibrant sunset gradients, featuring soft pebble-like surfaces and inviting, rustic-premium typography.

Pacing register: Generous for the storefront (appetizing, touch-friendly, spacious) and tight for the admin dashboard (structured, information-dense).

Surface treatment: Dark-mode-first. The background is a deep, earthy charcoal, with slightly lighter, warm-tinted charcoal surfaces for cards and popups to create subtle depth without relying heavily on shadows. 

Signature accent usage: Mint/teal is used as the primary functional color for navigation, active states, and success indicators. The warm red-to-orange gradient is reserved strictly for high-impact moments — primary CTAs (like "Add to cart" or "Top up"), the order countdown, and key highlights. 

Material and radius character: Soft, organic, and approachable. Elements use generous border radii (pebble-like cards, fully rounded pill buttons) to offset the dark palette and make the interface feel friendly and food-centric. Shadows are deep and subtle, while focus states use a soft mint glow.

Typographic decisions: A high-contrast pairing that balances premium organic food with modern usability. Headings use Fraunces (a warm, characterful serif with a slightly rustic, natural feel) to elevate the brand, while DM Sans serves as the clean, highly legible body face for product details and admin data.
